Effective date: 29 October 2026

Consent for sensitive data

Mood, journal, period, medicine and baby records are sensitive health data. Spada uploads them to your account only after you agree in the app. Without your consent they stay on your device and never leave it. You can change this at any time in Settings → Privacy & consent. If you withdraw it, the copies on our servers are removed the next time your device syncs, and new entries are no longer synced; what is already on your device stays there until you delete it or your account. The expenses and savings (balances, goals, deposits and withdrawals) you log are financial data. All of this is only shown back to you and is never used for advertising or shared with anyone else.

Device permissions

  • Calendar (optional). If you connect a device calendar in Notes, Spada reads its events to show them in the app. When you ask it to, Spada also adds events to that calendar, and edits or deletes events there. Events from your device calendar are not uploaded to our servers; on your device Spada only keeps the ID of the calendar you picked and of the events it created.
  • Photos (optional). Spada only asks for permission to save cards you create to your photo library. It cannot read your photo library; for meal photos you pick one photo at a time (see below).
  • Notifications (optional). Reminders are scheduled locally on your device. On Android, "Alarms & reminders" lets them arrive on time. We do not use push notification tokens.

Spada does not use your contacts or microphone.

Health data (Apple Health and Health Connect)

What. If you connect Apple Health (iPhone) or Health Connect (Android), Spada reads, with your permission: steps, workouts (type, start and end time), sleep (start and end time), and for each workout its distance, calories burned, average and maximum heart rate, elevation gained and route. Spada only reads this data. It never writes to Apple Health or Health Connect. Connecting also needs your consent for sensitive data in Settings → Privacy & consent.

Why. To show your activity in Spada, let you review and save workouts from your watch or other apps, show workout details and the route line, and tick habits you linked to a step or sleep goal.

Where it is stored. On your device only. It is never sent to our servers, never used for ads, never sold or shared, and never used to train AI models. Daily totals are kept for up to 60 days. Spada's use of information received from Health Connect adheres to the Health Connect Permissions policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Data from Apple Health is used only as described here and never for advertising.

How to delete it. Disconnect in Spada → Settings. This deletes the stored health data from your device right away. Saved workouts stay in your tracker until you delete them. You can also remove access in the iPhone Health app (Sharing → Apps → Spada) or in Android's Health Connect settings.

Location (walk and run recording)

What. When you start recording a walk or run, Spada uses your device's precise location (GPS) to record the route, distance and pace.

Why. Only to record the walk or run you started. Spada uses location only while a recording runs, including when the screen is off, and stops when you tap Stop. A notification (Android) or the location indicator (iPhone) is shown the whole time. Spada never uses location at other times, and never for ads or analytics.

Where it is stored. The route, the pace and elevation charts and the splits are saved with the walk on your device only. They are never sent to our servers. The walk itself (date, duration, distance, steps and your note) is saved in your Spada data like a walk you log by hand, and syncs to your account through Supabase so you can see it on your other devices.

How to delete it. Delete the walk in the app. Deleting your account deletes all synced data and the routes on this device. You can turn off location access at any time in your phone's settings; you can still log walks by hand.

Meal photos (Food & Drink)

What. You can add a photo of a meal by taking one with the camera or picking one from your library. Spada only accesses the photos you choose.

Why. To show the photo in your food diary.

Where it is stored. Spada shrinks the photo to a small size (256 pixels) and keeps it on your device only. Meal photos are never uploaded, synced or shared. Spada keeps up to 365 meal photos.

How to delete it. Remove the photo or delete the entry in the app. Uninstalling the app also removes them. Your original photo in your library is not changed.

Where your data is stored and who processes it

Your data is first saved on your device. Because you are signed in, a copy is synced to your account so you can use it on another device and restore it. We use these service providers (processors):

  • Supabase — sign-in and cloud storage of your app data, hosted on Amazon Web Services. Access is restricted so only your account can read or change your data.
  • Google — "Sign in with Google". Google handles your Google password; we never see it.
  • Apple — "Sign in with Apple". Apple handles your Apple Account password; we never see it. When you delete your account on iPhone, we ask Apple to revoke Spada's sign-in access.
  • Sentry (Functional Software, Inc.) — crash and error reporting.
  • PostHog (PostHog Inc.) — anonymous usage stats (opt-in on iPhone and in Europe; on by default on other Android phones, with an off switch in Settings). Hosted in the EU (Frankfurt). Location lookup from your IP address is turned off, and no session recording is used.

This version of Spada has no in-app purchases and no payment provider is active. If we add paid features, we will update this policy first.

Data sent between the app and these services is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Some providers are in, or may transfer data to, the United States. Those transfers rely on the provider's Standard Contractual Clauses and/or the EU–US Data Privacy Framework.

Legal bases (EU/EEA and UK users)

  • Contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR): account, profile, content and sync, to provide the service you asked for.
  • Explicit consent (Art. 9(2)(a) GDPR): mood, journal, period, medicine and baby records. Withdraw it at any time in Settings → Privacy & consent.
  • Consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR): marketing email, and anonymous usage stats on iPhone and in the EU/EEA, UK and Switzerland, only if you opt in. Usage stats are never on by default there.
  • Legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR): crash reports, to keep the app stable and secure.

How long we keep data

  • Account and synced app data: until you delete it or your account. Data on your device stays until you delete the account, uninstall the app or clear its storage; signing out alone does not erase it.
  • Crash reports: kept by Sentry for up to 90 days, then deleted automatically.
  • Anonymous usage stats: kept by PostHog for up to 12 months, then deleted.

Deleting your account

Open Settings → Delete account in the app. This permanently deletes your account and all your synced data from our servers and clears the data and reminders on that device. If you use Sign in with Apple on iPhone, Apple asks you to confirm so we can request revocation of the Apple sign-in from Apple. You can also ask us by email. See how to delete your account.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (including under the GDPR in the EU/EEA and UK), you can access, correct, delete or export your data, restrict or object to processing, and withdraw consent at any time:

  • Access and correct: see and edit your profile and everything you created directly in the app.
  • Delete: delete single entries in the app, or your whole account in Settings → Delete account.
  • Get a copy (access or export): email anggafauzan94@gmail.com from the address you sign in with. We send you a machine-readable copy of your data (JSON) within 30 days.
  • Anything else (restriction, objection, questions): email us.

We reply to every request within 30 days. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

Children

Spada is not directed at children. You must be at least 13 years old to use it, or the minimum age to consent to data processing in your country (for example 16 in Germany, 15 in France, 14 in Spain), unless a parent or guardian consents for you. Baby records you keep as a parent are your own data, not data collected from a child. If you believe a child has given us data, contact us and we will delete it.
