Delete your Spada account and data

Applies to the app "Spada: Habit Tracker" by MyVoyage. Effective date: 26 October 2026

Option 1: In the app (fastest)

  1. Open Spada and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm. If you use Sign in with Apple on iPhone, Apple asks you to confirm once more so we can ask Apple to revoke Spada's Apple sign-in.

Deletion happens right away. The app then signs you out and clears your data on that device.

Option 2: By email

If you can't use the app, email anggafauzan94@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my Spada account" from the email address of the Google or Apple account you use to sign in (with Apple "Hide My Email", tell us that relay address). We may ask you to confirm the request. We delete the account within 30 days and confirm by email.

What is deleted

  • Your account: email, name and profile details from Google or Apple, and your user ID. For Apple accounts we also request revocation of Spada's Apple sign-in from Apple.
  • All synced app data: profile (name, avatar, country, birth date, gender, height, waist), habits and streaks, quests, mood check-ins and journal entries, notes, to-dos, events you added in Spada, timers and timer sessions.
  • All tracker data: period, medicines, baby, pets, walks and runs, workouts, yoga, food and water, fasting and weight.
  • Money data: expenses, budgets and savings goals with their deposits and withdrawals.
  • Brain game progress, diamonds and Buddies, settings and your consent choices.
  • The app data stored on the device you delete from, plus its scheduled reminders.

What may be kept, and for how long

  • Events Spada added to your device calendar stay in that calendar; delete them there if you want.
  • Crash reports do not include your account ID and are deleted automatically after up to 90 days.
  • If you used Spada on another device, its local copy stays there until you uninstall the app or clear its storage on that device. Signing out alone does not erase it.

Deletion can't be undone. Your Google or Apple account itself is not affected.

Want to delete only some data instead? You can delete individual habits, entries, notes and to-dos inside the app at any time, and those changes sync to your account.
